## Risk Response Options

Use one of the following response strategies:

| Strategy | Description |
|---|---|
| Avoid | Change the plan to remove the risk |
| Mitigate | Reduce probability or impact |
| Transfer | Move responsibility or impact to another party |
| Accept | Acknowledge and monitor the risk |
| Escalate | Raise the risk to leadership or governance forum |

## Closure Criteria

A RAID item can be closed when:

- [ ] The risk has been mitigated, accepted, transferred, or is no longer relevant.
- [ ] The action has been completed and confirmed.
- [ ] The issue has been resolved and validated.
- [ ] The dependency has been delivered or is no longer required.
- [ ] Resolution notes are documented.
- [ ] Owner confirms closure.
- [ ] Business impact has been addressed, if applicable.
